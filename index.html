Questions people ask

Do I need to know anything about computers?

No. Just describe what you want in your own words. Photos, examples you like or a sketch on paper all help.

How do I pay?

Payment details are shared once your project is confirmed. Usually half at the start and half when you're happy with the result.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Posters include 2 rounds of changes; websites and games include changes until the agreed features work.

Will my website or game stay online?

Yes. It's hosted on a free service with its own link. If you want your own domain name (like yourshop.in), I can help set that up.

Who will I be talking to?

Enquiries are answered by Abdulla, with a parent reviewing every project before it's confirmed.
